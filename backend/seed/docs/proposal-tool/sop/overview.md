# Proposal & Estimate Tool — How to Use It

**Where:** https://proposals.wetreadwell.com
**Who:** anyone on the Treadwell team (sign in with your work Google account).

## What it does for you

You put in the basics of a job, and it hands you back a finished **estimate** and a
ready-to-send **proposal** — already saved to the team Dropbox. What used to be a lot of
copying between spreadsheets is now a few screens.

## Step by step

1. **Sign in** with your `@wetreadwell.com` Google account.
2. **Project info** — type the project name, address, contact, and a few notes about the job.
   - Want a head start? Tap **Auto-fill with AI** and it fills in the details for you from
     your notes. Always look it over and fix anything — it's a draft, you have the final say.
3. **Estimate** — enter the square footage and options. The prices fill in as you type and
   match the company's master pricing exactly. Tweak any number you need.
4. **Proposal** — review the wording (system, finish, scope, schedule, what's excluded).
   Your project info carries over automatically, so you're not typing things twice.
5. **Done** — download your estimate and proposal, or jump straight to the Dropbox folder
   it created for the job.

## Good to know

- Everyone shares one project list, so the whole team sees the same jobs.
- If Dropbox is briefly unavailable, you can still download your files right there — nothing
  is lost.
- **History** shows what was created and when. **Admin** (for admins) manages who has access.
- If a price ever looks wrong, flag it rather than forcing a number — the pricing is kept in
  sync with the master sheet.
