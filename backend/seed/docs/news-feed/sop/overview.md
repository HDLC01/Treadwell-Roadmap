# AI News Feed (Treadwell Radar) — How to Use It

**Where:** https://newsfeed.wetreadwell.com
**Who:** sales and leadership.

## What it does for you

It watches for **big construction projects** in our area — data centers, warehouses,
factories, hospitals, and the like — and surfaces the ones worth chasing, early. For each
one it gathers where it is, how far it is from the office, how far along it is, who's
building it, and who to contact. The goal: get Treadwell in front of the right people
**before** the job goes out to bid.

## What lands in your inbox

Every morning you get a short **"Top 5 hottest opportunities"** email — the best leads with
a one-line summary and a link to read more. Kyle gets it at 6 AM Central; Hanz gets it
around 7 PM his time (same list, just different clocks).

## Using the site

- **Feed** — the running list of opportunities, newest first. Filter by type, how close it
  is, or how hot it is, and search by name.
- **Radar (map)** — the office sits in the middle with the service-area rings around it, and
  every project is a dot. Use the **Hot / Warm / Cold** buttons to show just what you want.
- **Pipeline** — the ones you're tracking. Mark each as Watching, Pursuing, Won, or Passed,
  and jot notes.
- **Open a project** — see the team, the news behind it, and contact info.

## Good to know

- Hot, Warm, and Cold tell you how strong an opportunity looks for us.
- Contacts are gathered respectfully from public sources — we never pull anyone's private
  profiles.
- The system suggests leads; **you decide** who to reach out to and when.
